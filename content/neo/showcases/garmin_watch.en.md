This demo uses Machbase Neo to analyze one year of health data from a Garmin watch.

Key points

- Collect approximately 1.4 million data points from one runner's health and activity records.
- Install the app from the App Store and sign in with a Garmin account to collect data automatically every hour and retrieve the past year's records.
- Store heart rate, stress, step count, and activity data together in Machbase Neo.
- Visualize monthly trends and an activity calendar with 12 TQL-based dashboards.
- Compare 10 pairs of runs with similar distances and paces to observe a decrease in heart rate.
- Generate and execute SQL and TQL in AI chat, with charts displayed within the conversation.
- Connect data storage, dashboards, and AI analysis without additional tools.

Note: This is an unofficial tool with no affiliation with Garmin, designed for use only with your own account and data.
