This demo shows how to add tags in TagAnalyzer and analyze a selected time range with an FFT chart.

First, add the tags you want to analyze and choose either Aggregate or RAW mode.

Select the Selection button, then drag across the time range you want to analyze.

After selecting a range, a popup displays summary values such as the minimum, maximum, and average.

Select FFT to generate a chart for the selected range.

When the FFT window opens, the chart for the first tag appears automatically.

To analyze another tag, select it at the top of the window and press the play button to generate a new FFT chart.

You can also change Min Hz, Max Hz, and the 2D/3D settings as needed.
